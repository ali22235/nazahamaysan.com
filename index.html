Privacy Policy

Integrity Committee – Maysan Governorate

Effective Date: September 10, 2025

The Integrity Committee – Maysan Governorate (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information of users who interact with our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • User-submitted data: Complaints, requests, and reports submitted by citizens.
  • Contact information: Such as names, phone numbers, or email addresses, if provided voluntarily by users.
  • Attachments: Files, documents, or media uploaded by users to support their complaints or requests.
  • Technical information: Device type, operating system, and usage data (collected only to improve app performance).

2. How We Use Information

  • To process and review complaints, reports, and requests submitted by citizens.
  • To communicate with users regarding the status of their submissions.
  • To improve transparency and anti-corruption efforts.
  • To enhance app functionality and security.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

  • We do not sell, trade, or rent user information to third parties.
  • Data may only be shared with relevant authorized government entities as required by law and for official purposes.
  • Third-party services (e.g., Firebase for analytics and crash reporting) are used in compliance with Google Play policies.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect user data from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

5. User Rights

Users have the right to:

  • Access their personal information, where applicable.
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Request deletion of their submitted information, subject to applicable laws and official record-keeping requirements.
  • Ask questions about how their information is handled.

6. Data Retention

We retain submitted information only for as long as necessary to process complaints, requests, and reports, fulfill official purposes, comply with applicable laws, and maintain required government records.

7. Children's Privacy

The application is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your information, please contact the Integrity Committee – Maysan Governorate through the official contact channels provided within the application.
